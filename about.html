About TravelReady

TravelReady is an independently maintained travel-preparation resource. We publish guides and small browser-based tools for travelers who want a more reliable process for packing, verifying documents and handling itinerary changes.

Why we built this resource

Travel planning advice often blends what is universally helpful with rules that apply only to one nationality, destination or airline. We separate the two. Our articles explain repeatable methods—such as how to prepare an offline document backup or organize a carry-on—while pointing readers to official authorities for details that vary.

Who it is for

The site is for travelers planning ordinary leisure, work or family trips. The practical tools are intentionally simple and run in your browser. They are starting points for your own plan, not professional itinerary or eligibility assessments. We do not sell travel packages, process applications, make bookings or promise particular travel outcomes.

Our limits

TravelReady is not a government authority, embassy, airline, insurer, healthcare provider, financial adviser or immigration adviser. We do not determine eligibility to enter a country and cannot verify real-time laws or flight status. Rules, regulations and provider policies change. Readers are responsible for confirming them with the appropriate sources.

Editorial responsibility

Our guides are produced and maintained as TravelReady Editorial content. We document the difference between practical suggestions and authoritative requirements, use public references where appropriate and welcome corrections. We do not claim professional credentials we have not independently established. See our editorial standards and source directory.
