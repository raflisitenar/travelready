How to interpret the dates

These are prompts to start tasks, not legally mandated dates. Passport renewals, visa permissions, vaccination planning and certain bookings may require much more lead time depending on the traveler and destination. Start document verification as soon as the itinerary is under consideration. Never use this timeline to infer that an application submitted on a particular date will be accepted before departure.

Why a checklist needs a contingency step

Most rushed departures happen because several small tasks are pushed to the last day: searching for a charging cable, learning a baggage rule or locating an accommodation address. Splitting work into stages preserves time for questions to the relevant authority or carrier. Your final check should include live travel status, essential documents, medication needs and the first step after arrival.
