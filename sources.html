Official travel source directory

TravelReady intentionally avoids presenting a single worldwide passport, security or health rule. These resources are places to begin verification. The correct authority depends on the traveler, route and circumstances.

Passport, visa and entry

Begin with the destination and transit countries’ official immigration or embassy/consulate websites. The IATA Travel Centre helps travelers check documentation categories against their itinerary; always verify with the relevant authority and operating carrier.

Trip preparation and traveler documents

The U.S. State Department international travel checklist provides a useful example of official advice on document preparation, safety and insurance. U.S. government guidance is directed primarily to U.S. travelers and is not a substitute for your own country’s guidance.

Airport screening and baggage

The TSA travel checklist applies to relevant U.S. security procedures. Outside the U.S., use your departure airport’s security authority and the operating carrier’s current baggage policy. Airline baggage limits may change by fare, route and aircraft.

Health and destination risks

The CDC Travelers’ Health site offers destination-specific health information, while CDC’s pre-travel consultation guidance explains why individualized advice matters. Consult relevant authorities and qualified clinicians as appropriate.

How to use these sources

Record what you checked and when. If an itinerary changes, revisit the affected rules. When official pages disagree or are unclear, contact the responsible agency or carrier rather than relying on a cached search result or an automated summary. TravelReady does not certify or audit external websites.
