The information boundary

Trip rules are never universal.

Your citizenship, route, visa status, mode of transport and personal circumstances affect what documents you need. Rather than offering false certainty, we show you which questions to ask and where to verify the answers.

For international documentation, compare the relevant destination government and embassy information with your operating carrier. The IATA Travel Centre is a useful additional verification resource.

A useful three-step habit
  1. Write the exact itinerary and traveler details.
  2. Verify requirements with the relevant official authority.
  3. Record when you checked and revisit after changes.
