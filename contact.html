Contact TravelReady

Have a correction to suggest, spotted a broken link, or want to give feedback on a planning tool? We welcome specific and actionable messages.

Editorial contact

raflisitenar@gmail.com

Helpful details to include

Please include the URL of the page, the sentence or behavior you are reporting and, if possible, an official or reliable reference showing the correct information. For accessibility issues, share your browser, device and the action that failed. These details help us review the problem without guessing.

What we cannot provide

We cannot approve visas, confirm whether you may enter a country, check live airline availability, give medical advice, arrange insurance, or provide emergency help. For urgent matters, contact the airline, government authority, emergency service or professional relevant to your situation.

Please protect your privacy

Do not send passport numbers, payment card details, medical records or other sensitive personal information in an initial email. We can usually evaluate editorial feedback without such details.
