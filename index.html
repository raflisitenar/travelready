Travel day

Three things to keep simple.

A smooth departure is mostly about access: know where the essentials are, know your next step, and leave room for disruption.

01

Keep essentials reachable

  • Identification and required documents
  • Essential medication
  • Payment method
  • Phone and charging access
02

Check the live details

  • Departure status
  • Terminal, gate or platform
  • Local traffic/transit conditions
  • Carrier instructions
03

Protect some margin

  • Avoid overly tight connections where possible
  • Know a backup contact or route
  • Keep critical information available offline
  • Do not place every essential in checked luggage
Quick reference

Travel terms, decoded.

Know what the booking screen means.

Small terminology differences can affect baggage, connections, refunds and how much time you need.

LayoverA stop between segments of a journey before continuing onward.
Transit visaPermission some travelers may need while passing through a country; requirements vary by nationality and route.
Carry-onLuggage permitted in the passenger cabin, subject to carrier size, weight and item restrictions.
Checked baggageLuggage handed to the carrier for transport in the aircraft hold or equivalent storage.
Non-refundableA fare or booking condition limiting cash refunds; exact terms still depend on the specific booking rules.
FAQ

Useful planning questions.

How early should I start planning an international trip?

There is no universal lead time. Start early enough to verify passport, visa or entry rules, transportation, accommodation and any destination-specific requirements before non-flexible commitments become difficult to change.

How do I know what documents I need?

Use official government and embassy/consulate information for entry and transit rules, and confirm carrier requirements separately. Requirements can depend on nationality, destination, transit points and trip purpose.

Should important items go in checked luggage?

Keep items you may need during the journey—such as required identification, critical documents and essential medication—accessible and consistent with security/carrier rules.

Does this packing tool save my personal data?
