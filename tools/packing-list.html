How to use this checklist well

The generated items are prompts, not an authoritative list of legally permitted goods. Use the actual itinerary to decide what belongs in hand luggage, and check relevant airline, rail or road operator restrictions independently. For flight travel, cabin sizes, battery restrictions and liquid rules differ across providers and jurisdictions.

When reviewing the list, first decide which items are essential during a delay. Then adjust clothing to activities, weather and laundry access. Try the retrieval test described in our carry-on packing guide. The tool cannot know your medication requirements, destination regulations or personal preferences, so the additional-item field is available for details you choose to add privately.

Before you call the bag finished

  • Confirm baggage allowances for every operating carrier on the booking.
  • Check the destination forecast and any specialist-activity equipment needs.
  • Keep required identification and important travel information accessible where permitted.
  • Use the print view as a last check, not as a replacement for checking official restrictions.
