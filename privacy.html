Privacy policy

This notice explains the information used when you visit TravelReady and operate our planning tools. Contact: raflisitenar@gmail.com.

Planning tool data

The packing builder stores your selections, checked items and custom packing entries in your own browser using localStorage when supported. The departure planner performs date calculations in your browser and does not submit your selected departure date to us. The website does not require a TravelReady account or accept uploaded passports, IDs or medical records. Browser storage can be cleared by you using the tool controls or browser settings. Someone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view locally saved planner information.

Hosting and technical information

As with typical hosted websites, the hosting and delivery infrastructure may process routine technical request data such as IP address, browser characteristics and request logs for operations, security and troubleshooting. Retention and access depend on the hosting provider’s actual practices. We do not promise that ordinary server logs are absent.

Google AdSense and cookies

TravelReady includes Google AdSense code. If advertising is enabled, Google and advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure and help personalize advertising, where applicable. See Google’s advertising technology information and Google Ad Settings. Where consent is required by law, the site operator must configure an appropriate consent mechanism before enabling applicable personalized ad processing.

Email communications

If you email us, your message and contact address are received for the purpose of addressing the inquiry. Please do not include sensitive identity or payment information unless it is genuinely necessary; most feedback requires none.

Your choices and questions

You can clear local packing data using the Reset control or your browser’s storage settings, adjust cookie preferences where provided, and use browser privacy controls. For privacy questions, write to raflisitenar@gmail.com. We may update this notice as tools or service configurations change.
