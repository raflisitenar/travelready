Editorial standards

Our goal is to publish advice that a reader can put into action while making it clear where general planning guidance ends and official requirements begin.

How guides are structured

Each guide begins with a focused traveler problem. We describe the sequence of decisions, illustrate tradeoffs with realistic examples and end with a short action checklist. We do not publish long pages whose only purpose is to repeat keywords, and we do not present a generated checklist as a source of visa or security requirements.

Sources and factual boundaries

For immigration, travel documents, security or health issues, we direct readers toward the relevant official government, carrier or public-health resources. The IATA Travel Centre may be used as a cross-check. These sources can change after we publish. A citation does not imply that every reader faces the same rules or that the cited organization endorses TravelReady.

Review and updates

Articles show a publication and review date. A date indicates editorial work on the material and does not mean we monitor every country’s rules continuously. Where a fact cannot be safely generalized, we explicitly ask the reader to verify it. We revise pages when we identify factual errors, broken links, confusing statements or meaningful changes in our own tools.

Corrections

Email raflisitenar@gmail.com with a page URL and supporting details. We review the claim against the relevant primary source, correct material errors and update the displayed editorial date when substantive changes are made.

Independence and advertising

TravelReady is an independent educational site. It may use advertising to support operation, but promotional services do not determine the substance of our guides. We do not claim independent testing or first-hand travel experience that we have not documented. Educational content is not individualized legal, financial or medical advice.
